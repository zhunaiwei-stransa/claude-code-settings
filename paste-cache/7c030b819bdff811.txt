**Go:** No credit card deletion logic exists in `cancelservice` or the cancel usecase.

**Conditions for credit card deletion (PHP):**
1. Office contract has telemedicine + credit requirement enabled
2. The cancelled reservation is a telemedicine reservation with credit-required menu
3. The patient has no other future telemedicine+credit reservations
4. The patient's registered card is a one-time card (`is_one_time = 1`)

**Impact:**
- Telemedicine offices with credit card requirements: one-time cards will NOT be cleaned up when the associated reservation is cancelled via Go
- The stale card data is not harmful but won't be automatically removed
- This is a best-effort operation in PHP (failure doesn't fail the cancel)

**Assessment:** Likely intentional omission if telemedicine credit card cleanup is planned as a separate migration. Confirm with team whether this feature is needed.
